## Privacy and qualification limits

Only safe textual artifacts are included: source/pins/reviews, redacted logs,
JUnit, progress, runner receipts, source/corpus manifests and safe dependency
identity. Raw tests.json, package-origin.json, environment-bearing
readiness/start/stop JSON, credential files, private-home content, installed
runtime/dependency contents, caches and database state are excluded. Recorded
file paths and digests are metadata. Bounded high-confidence secret-pattern
checks are not universal proof against arbitrary unrecognized secrets.

Safe readiness/stop logs and corpus-isolation evidence are preserved. Numeric
wrapper/stop exits are coordinator-reported where noted by the independent
reviewers; raw environment-bearing JSON was not opened to manufacture those
attestations. This archive restores selected evidence, not a working lab.

No database, test, product/harness module, Git, network transfer, credential or
new environment action occurs during this data-only build. Full installed-suite,
unrelated application, production-provider, performance, cancellation/concurrency,
fresh-cluster, signing and release qualification remain outside scope. All seven
gates remain UNKNOWN. Publication requires separate review and authorization.
